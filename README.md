HAXLR8 3.0 is a 24-hour national-level prototyping hackathon organized by the **Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (EMITERS)** at **Maharaja Institute of Technology Mysore (MIT Mysore)**.

---

## 🚀 Event Summary

- **Event:** HAXLR8 3.0 National Level Hackathon
- **Institution:** Maharaja Institute of Technology Mysore (MIT Mysore)
- **Organizing Body:** Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering & EMITERS
- **Dates:** November 6 – 7, 2026
- **Format:** 24-Hour Non-stop Prototyping Marathon
- **Prize Pool:** ₹33,333 Cash Bounty
- **Team Size:** 3 – 4 Members (Inter-college squads permitted)
- **Official Contact:** `haxlr8ecemitm@gmail.com`

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Three.js / Canvas
- **Backend / Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Realtime, Auth, Storage)
- **Media Delivery:** Cloudinary & Supabase Realtime Storage
- **Mailing Service:** Gmail SMTP & Nodemailer Serverless Handler
- **Deployment:** Vercel
